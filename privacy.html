Privacy Policy

App: MHS Date & Time
Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

MHS Date & Time does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location or any other personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What is stored on your device

Your calendar reminders and the clock time you choose to set are saved only in your device's local storage. We cannot see this information and it is never uploaded to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

The app makes no network requests for tracking or analytics. No advertising. No tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing of data. No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
